  • Capital Markets Practice

Capital creates the greatest value when its structure, cost, investor base, and timing support the business plan. Sett & Lucas advises companies, founders, and shareholders on private equity, debt, and hybrid financing, helping determine the right capital structure, investor base, transaction terms, and funding strategy for long-term flexibility and growth.

Our work begins with your operating plan, capital requirements, cash-generation profile, existing capitalization, and future financing needs. We evaluate the trade-offs across dilution, leverage, governance, cost of capital, liquidity, and financial flexibility to determine the structure best suited to your objectives.

  • Debt and Private Credit
  • Venture Debt and Non-Dilutive Capital

Equity and Growth Capital

When you are seeking minority or growth equity, we help identify investors whose capital, investment horizon, sector expertise, governance expectations, and strategic capabilities align with your growth plan.

Our work includes valuation analysis, financial modeling, investor mapping, positioning, marketing materials, management preparation, term-sheet evaluation, and negotiation. Where appropriate, we also help structure the balance between primary capital for the business and secondary liquidity for existing shareholders.

Debt and Private Credit

We help you evaluate private debt solutions including senior secured facilities, unitranche financing, acquisition financing, asset-based lending, ARR-based facilities, subordinated debt, mezzanine capital, and other bespoke credit structures.

We assess debt capacity, cash interest burden, amortization, covenant headroom, collateral requirements, maturity profile, and refinancing risk to structure financing that meets your immediate requirements without unnecessarily constraining future growth or strategic options.

Venture Debt and Non-Dilutive Capital

For venture-backed and high-growth businesses, debt can extend runway, support specific growth initiatives, and reduce the amount of equity required in the near term.

We assess whether venture debt is appropriate based on recurring revenue quality, growth, burn rate, liquidity, equity sponsorship, and the expected path to the next financing or liquidity event. We then advise on facility sizing, pricing, warrants, covenants, repayment terms, and overall capital structure.

Structured and Hybrid Capital

When conventional equity or senior debt does not fully meet your objectives, structured and hybrid capital can provide greater flexibility.

We advise on preferred equity, convertible securities, subordinated instruments, and other bespoke structures tailored around return requirements, downside protection, governance, and future liquidity.

By modeling these securities across different operating and exit scenarios, we help you understand the true economic cost of capital, including dilution, cash obligations, ownership outcomes, and future financing implications.
