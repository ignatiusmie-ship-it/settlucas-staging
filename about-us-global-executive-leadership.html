The people driving us forward

Our leadership council is responsible for vision, strategy, execution and operations. It includes managing partners responsible for service lines in each region.

Charles Breed: Managing Director SaaS Practice - Americas

Charles Breed is a seasoned M&A expert with over two decades of experience at Cisco, Symantec, SAP and KKR. He has closed over 75 international transactions worth $28B in complex mergers, acquisitions, and divestitures.

Charles has spent his career as a VP of Corporate Development, responsible for Buy-Side M&A, IP in-bound licensing, Corporate Venture Investing, Divestitures, and International Joint Ventures. His technical area of expertise has been Cybersecurity, Identity Access Management, BOT Management, AI/ML, Subscription Billing, Enterprise Performance Management, eCommerce, Social Media & Creative Content Generation and Marketplaces. Charles has been in the Software-as-a-Service industry since it first began, working on PKI, secure e-mail, VPN, and network security in general. He has spent the majority of his career in San Francisco Bay Area, working at Cisco Systems on CiscoSecure and various M&A transactions, and has been continually active in various IETF working groups, such as IPsec, PKIX and the chairperson of the Open PGP working group. The Information Security Magazine featured his PKI article as the cover story, and Charles is the author and creator of the industry’s de facto “Cryptographic Reference Chart”, “Security Threats Reference Chart”, and “Network Security Reference Chart” with over 350,000 in print, translated into three languages. Mr. Breed is on the board of Directors for five prominent companies, holds a degree in Business Administration and Management Information Systems from the University of San Francisco, a master’s degree, Certificate programs from Stanford, has taught a graduate course on M&A at UC Berkeley, and is a Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) from ISC2.

Tyler Block: Managing Director - Americas

Tyler is part of the US operations and leads Software and Services sectors for the US market. He brings nearly 20 years of M&A investment banking and top-level corporate strategy consulting experience to the team.

Tyler previously led deal teams for Redwood Capital, a New York-based middle market M&A firm, and Gemini Partners, a Los Angeles-based middle market M&A firm. He has successfully completed numerous sell-side M&A transactions and has been a winner of The M&A Advisor’s Consumer Services Deal of the Year Award. Prior to that, Tyler was a management strategy consultant at Accenture. Mr. Block is a graduate of Harvard, having completed his undergraduate studies in Economics and his graduate studies in Finance.

Jim Hageman: Director - Americas

Jim Hageman currently represents Sett & Lucas as a Director of Investment Banking primarily working with Strategic Buyers and Private Equity Groups.

Jim has over 20 years of experience in M&A, Corporate Development, and Information Technology working with companies in North America. Prior to joining Sett & Lucas, Jim worked with Generational Equity as a Sr. Business Advisor, providing M&A capabilities to small to mid-market companies. In addition, Jim worked with a number of Information Technology companies, including CompuCom, Cap Gemini, Decision One, and Enter in the areas of Corporate Development and Technology offerings.

Muthukumar Velumani (MK): Managing Partner - APAC

Muthukumar (MK) manages investment banking operations and supports transaction advisory services with a focus on the Asia Pacific markets.

He is based in the India office of Sett & Lucas in Chennai. With over two decades of senior operating experience with global financial institutions, MK is a private placement, M&A, book building and due diligence expert. One of the major highlights of his career was the successful merger and integration of the Royal Bank of Scotland and ABN Amro – at RBS, MK was transition and integration manager and instrumental in the successful transition of global investment banking processes in the target operating model, post-acquisition. He has worked across treasury, mortgage and interest rate derivatives, driving change in ABN Amro, and was also instrumental in creating a platform to migrate the more complex investment banking operations by successfully leading the pilot for the complex Marco Polo project. MK holds a post graduate diploma in finance, is a chartered financial analyst, and holds a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering.

Dominic Marlow: Managing Partner – UK

Dominic Marlow manages the investment banking practice for the EMEA region based out of London.

Dominic is a veteran deal maker in the mid-market, with vast experience across numerous sectors, having completed over 50 successful transactions over the past five years. He is a renowned executive coach who works with several privately held and private equity held company CEOs in London, combining board level and executive experience with corporate finance to help CEOs achieve shareholder value maximization. Dominic has achieved a high level of success working with IT services and enterprise software companies in the Thames Valley region, facilitating strategic joint ventures and long term exit planning. Living in Oxford, he is located at the heart of the UK technology community, and has mentored several early and growth stage companies over the last decade. Dominic has been a professional dealmaker since 2005, prior to that holding several senior strategic and corporate development roles working for GKN, Britvic and executive consulting roles with Nestle and Coca Cola.

James Battison: VP - Investment Banking - UK

James Battison works directly with UK and European clients to help unlock value through strategic Mergers & Acquisitions.

James represents Sett & Lucas as VP of Investment Banking across the UK and Europe. Having spent the previous 5 years within the Sett & Lucas Group, James stepped into this role as VP of S&L’s investment banking practice in 2022. James is a subject matter expert in tech enabled services and has previously worked as Chief Commercial Officer for a UK-based RPA and Intelligent Automation company, where he was pivotal in scaling the business from a bootstrapped start-up to a successful tech services organisation serving mid-market customers in both the UK and US. James works closely with CEOs right from their first engagement with Sett & Lucas, rapidly gauging their wants and needs while deeply understanding the business and its assets.

Prabhu Antony: Non-Executive Director (NED)

Prabhu Antony is the founder of Sett & Lucas and currently a Non Executive Director. He has over $10B in M&A, Public listings, private equity and corporate financing transactions.

Prabhu Antony is the Founder and CIO of Scieniti, an Alternate Asset Manager and Advisory firm. He previously co-founded Sett & Lucas, the advisory arm under the Scieniti umbrella. Today, Scieniti manages a diversified portfolio across Public Equities (IPOs, SPACs, Hedge strategies), Private Equity, Venture Capital, and Real Assets, partnering with a distinguished network of global co-investors including Harvard Endowment, META, SoftBank, and Tiger Global in private markets, and Mastercard, Alberta Investment Management, Jane Street, EQT, and UBS in public markets. Under Prabhu’s leadership, Scieniti oversees over $1B in AUM, spanning 32 countries, 46 offices, 11 industries, and employing 1,500 professionals of 28 nationalities. Earlier in his career, Prabhu led corporate development at Oracle and built deep expertise in cross-border M&A across the US–Asia corridor, closing $10B+ in transactions. His dealmaking track record has been recognized globally with honors including Investment Banker of the Year, Top 40 Under 40, and Best Cross-Border Deal of the Year at the Global M&A Forum. He holds a Bachelor’s in Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering, an MBA, and a Postgraduate Diploma in International Business, and is an alumnus of the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, Anna University, and Madras University.
