Industry expertise

In technology M&A, sector knowledge is a prerequisite. Founders, investors, and acquirers quickly recognize whether an adviser genuinely understands the business models, economics, risks, and competitive dynamics that define a market.

Sett & Lucas has built its expertise through direct transaction experience across seven technology sectors. We focus on the issues that materially affect positioning, valuation, transaction structure, and execution in each market.

Our approach is sector-specific rather than formulaic. The factors that drive value in software are different from those that matter in technology services, fintech, AI, or healthcare, and our advice reflects those differences.

Enterprise Software & Cybersecurity

We advise software and security businesses where recurring revenue, critical workflows and defensible technology drive value.

Read story

Enterprise software and cybersecurity are being reshaped by AI, cloud adoption, platform consolidation, machine identities, and evolving security requirements.

The central transaction question is durability. Buyers and investors want to understand whether recurring revenue, ARR growth, retention, workflow criticality, product differentiation, security relevance, and competitive positioning can be sustained as technology evolves.

Our work focuses on separating durable software economics and strategic product relevance from short-term growth. We position businesses around recurring revenue quality, customer dependence, platform depth, product roadmap, defensibility, and the strategic importance of the problem being solved.

Our coverage includes:

  • Application and vertical-market software
  • Cloud, infrastructure and developer software
  • Cybersecurity platforms and managed security services
  • Identity, access, machine identity, data security and security operations
  • Governance, risk, compliance, cyber resilience and AI security

Technology Services & Digital Engineering

We advise technology services firms helping enterprises modernise platforms, build digital products and manage complex technology.

Read story

Technology services firms are being pushed to prove that growth comes from differentiated capability rather than simply additional headcount.

The key transaction challenge is scalability. Buyers assess client concentration, revenue visibility, utilisation, delivery mix, specialist talent, platform partnerships, proprietary accelerators, AI capability, and whether margins can improve as the business grows.

We help clients distinguish strategic capability from commoditized delivery. The focus is on demonstrating where vertical expertise, engineering depth, recurring managed services, reusable IP, delivery leverage, and embedded client relationships create value beyond traditional labour-based models.

Our coverage includes:

  • Digital product engineering and software development services
  • Cloud transformation, platform engineering and managed services
  • Data engineering, analytics and AI engineering
  • Enterprise platforms and systems integration
  • Quality engineering, DevOps, automation and cybersecurity services

Commerce & Digital Experience

We advise businesses at the intersection of commerce, customer experience, marketing technology and digital product engagement.

Read story

Commerce and customer experience are evolving rapidly as AI, first-party data, personalisation and platform consolidation reshape digital growth.

The transaction challenge is proving measurable commercial relevance. Businesses must demonstrate that value extends beyond project work and that their capabilities influence customer acquisition, conversion, retention, loyalty, or revenue growth.

We help clients frame the connection between creative capability, technology, data, and commercial outcomes. Particular emphasis is placed on durable client relationships, recurring or retainer-based revenue, proprietary technology, platform expertise, and evidence of measurable impact on customer outcomes.

Our coverage includes:

  • Digital commerce, marketplaces and commerce enablement
  • Customer experience, UX/UI and digital product design
  • MarTech, customer data and personalisation solutions
  • Performance marketing, analytics and growth services
  • Creative, content and technology-enabled brand experience services

Professional Services & Consulting

We advise specialist consulting firms built on expertise, trusted client relationships and repeatable intellectual capital.

Read story

AI, automation, and new delivery models are changing how professional services firms create, deliver, and scale expertise.

The central transaction question is institutionalisation. Buyers want to understand how much value resides in individual professionals and how much has been embedded into the wider business through recurring client relationships, methodologies, intellectual property, data, and technology-enabled delivery.

We help firms demonstrate that expertise can scale beyond individual rainmakers. Leadership depth, repeat business, revenue visibility, consultant productivity, proprietary methodologies, delivery leverage, and the ability to convert specialist knowledge into repeatable capability become central to positioning.

Our coverage includes:

  • Strategy, transformation and management consulting
  • Technology, data and digital advisory
  • Financial, risk, compliance and regulatory consulting
  • Human capital, workforce and organisational advisory
  • Specialist research, intelligence and other knowledge-led services

Artificial Intelligence & Data Infrastructure

We advise companies building the software, data platforms and infrastructure that enable enterprises to deploy AI at scale.

Read story

AI companies operate in a market where technology, differentiation, and valuation frameworks are evolving rapidly.

The transaction challenge is proving defensibility. As models and features become more accessible, value increasingly depends on proprietary data, workflow integration, deployment maturity, infrastructure, switching costs, intellectual property, inference economics, and mission-critical use cases.

We help clients identify where durable value genuinely resides and communicate that clearly. The focus is on separating value from capabilities that can be replicated, commoditised, or absorbed into broader platforms.

Our coverage includes:

  • Enterprise AI applications and agentic workflow platforms
  • Agent infrastructure, orchestration, evaluation and observability
  • Data engineering, integration, analytics and real-time data platforms
  • Machine learning infrastructure, model deployment and inference tooling
  • Data governance, privacy, quality, security and metadata solutions
  • Cloud, compute and AI infrastructure

Fintech, Payments & Digital Assets

We advise technology-led financial businesses reshaping how money is moved, managed, accessed and embedded into digital products.

Read story

Fintech is evolving as payments, embedded finance, stablecoins, tokenisation, and digital infrastructure increasingly converge.

The transaction challenge is balancing growth with economic and regulatory quality. Transaction volumes alone are not enough; investors and acquirers examine take rates, recurring revenue, customer acquisition economics, fraud and loss performance, regulatory exposure, capital intensity, scalability, and distribution strength.

Our work focuses on showing how the business creates durable value within the financial system. We assess where infrastructure, licences, data, network effects, distribution, or embedded customer relationships create an advantage that can support stronger economics and strategic relevance.

Our coverage includes:

  • Payments, merchant acquiring and payment infrastructure
  • Banking, lending and embedded-finance technology
  • Capital markets, wealth management and financial-data platforms
  • InsurTech, RegTech, risk and compliance technology
  • Stablecoins, tokenisation, custody and digital-asset infrastructure

Healthcare Technology & Life Sciences

We advise technology and services businesses improving how healthcare is delivered, administered, researched and commercialised.

Read story

Healthcare technology sits at the intersection of technology, regulation, clinical outcomes, reimbursement, data, and stakeholder adoption.

The transaction challenge is connecting commercial performance with clinical and workflow relevance. Buyers must understand regulatory requirements, reimbursement exposure, interoperability, evidence, customer concentration, product adoption, proprietary data, and whether the solution produces measurable outcomes.

We help clients translate technical and clinical capabilities into a clear economic and strategic case. The focus is on demonstrating workflow criticality, adoption, regulatory defensibility, data value, and the extent to which the product improves efficiency, outcomes, or decision-making across the healthcare ecosystem.

Our coverage includes:

  • Healthcare software, digital health and tech-enabled services
  • Provider, payer and revenue cycle management technology
  • Clinical workflow, patient engagement and care-delivery platforms
  • Life sciences software, data, research and technology-enabled services
  • Diagnostics, laboratory informatics and selected life sciences tools
