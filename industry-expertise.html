Industry expertise

One of our biggest differentiators is the depth of industry expertise. The focus is on business models that have evolved around information technology, digital transformation and enterprise software.

Sett & Lucas’ proprietary scorecard process is a testament to this.

The rapid evolution in the technology and industrials markets has led to a rise in transactions in the sector. Sett & Lucas prides itself as a leading advisor to firms in the following industry verticals.

Enterprise software & SaaS

The enterprise software market has evolved with the advent of software as a service (SaaS).

Read story

The enterprise software market has undergone rapid change with the advent of the software as a service (SaaS) model, made possible by advances in cloud computing. While Sett & Lucas has traditionally represented high growth enterprise software firms over the years, the firm has adapted to the emerging technology trends around SaaS, such as artificial intelligence (AI), cyber security, autonomous driving technology, robotics and unified communications. As early adopters, Sett & Lucas is proud of their domain expertise and transaction experience. The work in this practice area can be classified into: SaaS and on-premise software, vertical industry focussed software, and Next Gen technologies (artificial intelligence, robotics and machine learning).
