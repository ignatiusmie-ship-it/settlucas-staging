Public markets introduce a different standard of preparation, disclosure, valuation, governance, and investor engagement. Sett & Lucas advises companies and shareholders on capital-markets decisions where positioning, timing, transaction structure, and the quality of the investor base can materially influence the outcome.

Our Capital Markets Practice connects corporate finance analysis with public-market thinking. We help clients understand how investors are likely to evaluate the business, prepare for market scrutiny, assess financing and liquidity alternatives, and execute transactions with a clear view of valuation, dilution, ownership, and longer-term market access.

IPO Readiness and Strategic Preparation

An IPO is both a capital event and a fundamental transition in how a company is evaluated. Preparation extends beyond the transaction timetable to financial reporting, governance, operating visibility, management readiness, investor communications, and the ability to support a credible long-term public-market investment case.

We work with management and shareholders to assess readiness, identify gaps, benchmark the company against relevant public peers, and evaluate transaction size, primary versus secondary shares, dilution, use of proceeds, and the milestones required before approaching the market.

Equity Story, Valuation and Investor Positioning

Public-market valuation depends not only on financial performance, but also on the credibility and visibility of future results. Investors assess growth, margins, cash generation, market opportunity, competitive positioning, customer quality, and management execution relative to comparable companies.

We translate the operating plan into a clear, evidence-based equity story and benchmark the company against relevant public companies and transactions. This analysis helps establish valuation expectations, identify the metrics investors are likely to focus on, and determine the operational milestones that can strengthen positioning.

Pre-IPO Positioning and Crossover Capital

Some companies benefit from strengthening their capital base and institutional investor profile before entering the public markets. Pre-IPO or crossover financing can provide growth capital, support acquisitions, improve balance-sheet flexibility, or create additional time to establish the scale and reporting history expected by public investors.

We help clients assess whether a pre-IPO financing strengthens the eventual public-market path and advise on transaction size, valuation, investor selection, structure, dilution, and the potential implications for a future IPO.

SPAC and Alternative Public-Market Transactions

A SPAC transaction can provide an alternative route to the public markets where transaction structure, valuation certainty, financing, shareholder composition, and execution timing differ materially from a conventional IPO.

We advise companies and shareholders on evaluating whether a SPAC transaction is appropriate, assessing potential counterparties, transaction valuation, ownership and dilution, sponsor economics, PIPE financing requirements, redemption risk, shareholder alignment, and the implications for the combined company after closing.

Our focus is to evaluate a SPAC transaction against other available strategic and capital-markets alternatives so that the chosen route supports both the immediate transaction objectives and the company’s longer-term position as a public company.

Follow-On Equity and Shareholder Liquidity

For listed companies, additional equity capital can support acquisitions, organic growth, balance-sheet strengthening, and other strategic priorities. Existing shareholders may also seek liquidity through secondary offerings or block transactions.

We advise on transaction rationale, size, timing, valuation, primary versus secondary components, investor positioning, and ownership implications. Our focus is to balance immediate financing or liquidity objectives with dilution, trading dynamics, shareholder composition, and continued access to the public markets.

PIPE and Equity-Linked Transactions

PIPEs and equity-linked securities can provide flexible capital when a conventional marketed offering is not the preferred solution. Depending on the circumstances, these transactions may involve common equity, preferred securities, convertible instruments, or other negotiated structures.

We evaluate pricing, dilution, governance, conversion economics, investor rights, market signaling, and future financing implications to determine whether the structure supports the company’s broader capital-markets objectives.

Market Timing and Transaction Strategy

Capital-markets windows can change quickly as sector sentiment, comparable-company performance, volatility, issuance activity, investor risk appetite, and company-specific milestones evolve.

We use market, valuation, and transaction analysis to help clients determine when execution conditions are attractive, what preparation should occur before launch, and how transaction structure and investor positioning should respond to prevailing market conditions.

Across private-to-public transitions and transactions by listed companies, our focus remains consistent: rigorous preparation, credible positioning, informed valuation, and disciplined execution designed to support durable access to the public markets.
