For founders, shareholders, and financial sponsors, a sale is a defining milestone. Sett & Lucas provides independent, end-to-end sell-side M&A advice from preparation through closing, combining sector insight, global buyer relationships, valuation discipline, and competitive process management to position businesses effectively, preserve confidentiality, and maximize transaction outcomes for shareholders globally.

Our work begins entirely with your specific objectives. We transform your unique goals into a customized transaction process designed around your priorities rather than forcing you into a rigid, standard auction format. This methodical, milestone-driven preparation is your direct path to securing a premium valuation.

Strategic Positioning and Transaction Readiness

The strongest sale processes are built long before buyers are contacted. You work alongside our senior execution team to unpack historical performance, normalize earnings where appropriate, test your operating model, isolate key value drivers, and clearly frame the future growth opportunity. This rigorous preparation helps your business surface and resolve potential transaction issues early. It ensures your equity story is anchored in clear financial evidence rather than mere aspiration.

We develop custom transaction materials that clearly communicate why your business matters, where it can scale next, and why a particular acquirer can create meaningful strategic and financial value. For technology, software, digital, or services businesses, we articulate critical value drivers including:

  • Recurring revenue quality and net retention rates
  • Customer concentration and value metrics analysis
  • Proprietary intellectual property and product or solution roadmaps
  • Strategic positioning and geographic scalability
  • Opportunities for cross-selling or margin expansion

Valuation and Shareholder Outcome Analysis

Your ultimate value is shaped by far more than a headline market multiple. Buyer synergies, strategic scarcity, growth durability, competitive positioning, capital requirements, deal structure, and market timing all materially influence your final outcome.

We evaluate valuation through relevant public benchmarks, recent transaction data, and buyer-specific economics. We use this analysis to determine how your company should be positioned and how market outreach should be sequenced.

To preserve optionality, we assess alternative strategic outcomes alongside a traditional full sale. You receive clear financial modeling for majority investments, minority recapitalizations, strategic partnerships, or other structured solutions, giving shareholders a clear view of available paths before committing to a single route.

Buyer Identification and Global Market Mapping

A successful transaction depends on reaching buyers with both the strategic rationale and financial capacity to transact. You gain access to our proprietary market mapping across strategic acquirers and financial sponsors globally, including non-obvious buyers outside the conventional competitive peer set. We assess each potential buyer for strategic alignment, acquisition history, available capital, decision-making speed, and the premium they may be able to underwrite.

For cross-border situations, we tailor positioning and market engagement to the local commercial, cultural, and regulatory environment while maintaining a consistent transaction narrative. This expands competitive interest without compromising confidentiality or process discipline.

Confidential Marketing and Competitive Process Management

We manage outreach, non-disclosure agreements, management presentations, indications of interest, and subsequent bidding rounds with one objective: preserving your optionality while increasing competitive tension.

We help you evaluate offers not only on headline price, but also on form of consideration, financing certainty, conditionality, regulatory risk, diligence requirements, management expectations, and closing certainty.

As negotiations progress, we help shareholders understand the true economics and trade-offs embedded in each proposal while maintaining momentum across parallel workstreams.

Due Diligence Coordination, Signing, and Closing Support

Due diligence is where transaction preparation is tested. We coordinate with management, legal, tax, accounting, and other advisors to organize the data room, anticipate detailed buyer diligence, and resolve issues without allowing the process to lose momentum.

We support negotiations on the financial and commercial aspects of definitive transaction documentation, helping shareholders understand how changes in structure affect value, risk allocation, and transaction certainty.

From the first discussion of shareholder objectives through signing and closing, our focus remains consistent: preparation, informed choice, competitive tension, and execution discipline designed to pursue the best available outcome.
