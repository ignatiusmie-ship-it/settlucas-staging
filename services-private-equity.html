Sett & Lucas provides strategic and transaction advisory to private equity firms, portfolio companies, founders, and management teams across acquisitions, investments, recapitalizations, capital raising, liquidity events, and exits. Our approach combines sector insight, valuation discipline, sponsor relationships, and execution expertise across the private capital investment lifecycle.

Private equity transactions bring together capital, ownership, strategy, and execution. Sett & Lucas advises clients across the investment lifecycle, helping them evaluate opportunities, pursue acquisitions, optimize portfolio company capital structures, and prepare for eventual liquidity.

Our role is transaction advisory rather than fund management. We bring an independent perspective to situations where ownership, control, financing, value creation, and exit strategy must be considered together.

Financial Sponsor Coverage and Transaction Origination

We support financial sponsors with sector mapping, investment theme development, target identification, and transaction origination aligned with defined investment mandates and return objectives.

Our sector-led approach is particularly relevant in technology, software, IT services, and other knowledge-driven businesses where investment decisions depend on revenue quality, customer durability, margins, scalability, intellectual property, talent, and long-term value-creation potential.

Portfolio Company M&A and Strategic Acquisitions

For portfolio companies pursuing platform expansion or add-on acquisitions, we help translate strategic priorities into an actionable M&A agenda.

Our work includes target screening, valuation, approach strategy, transaction structuring, negotiation, and financing considerations. We assess potential acquisitions for strategic fit, integration logic, cross-sell potential, margin expansion, geographic reach, and their contribution to the sponsor’s broader value-creation plan.

Majority and Minority Investments

We advise on majority and minority private equity investments where ownership, valuation, governance, and future liquidity must be carefully balanced.

Our work includes transaction evaluation, valuation analysis, ownership structuring, shareholder considerations, governance rights, and negotiation. We help clients assess how different investment structures affect control, strategic decision-making, capital allocation, and future exit options.

Growth Equity and Recapitalizations

Growth equity and recapitalization transactions can provide capital for expansion, acquisitions, or shareholder liquidity without necessarily requiring a full change of control.

We help clients evaluate valuation, dilution, governance, liquidity rights, leverage, and future participation. Where multiple structures are available, we model the alternatives to show how each option affects ownership, proceeds, risk, and long-term strategic flexibility.

Sponsor-to-Sponsor and Secondary Transactions

As companies mature within private ownership, another financial sponsor may become the natural next owner. These transactions require a clear articulation of what has been achieved, what remains to be underwritten, and why the business can support another investment cycle.

We position the company against the priorities of relevant sponsors, manage the transaction process, and evaluate proposals across valuation, financing, execution certainty, management alignment, and transaction structure.

Portfolio Company Capital Raising

Portfolio companies may require additional capital during the holding period to fund acquisitions, accelerate growth, refinance existing obligations, or support strategic initiatives.

We advise on equity, private credit, and structured capital alternatives, helping sponsors and management teams assess financing capacity, cost of capital, dilution, leverage, covenant flexibility, and the impact of new capital on the overall investment case.

Exit Planning and Sell-Side M&A

Exit preparation should begin well before a formal sale process is launched. We work with sponsors, founders, and management teams to identify the value drivers future buyers will test, strengthen the supporting financial and strategic evidence, and determine the appropriate timing and route to liquidity.

Depending on the company and market conditions, alternatives may include a strategic sale, sponsor-to-sponsor transaction, recapitalization, minority liquidity event, or future capital-markets transaction. We help evaluate these options and execute the selected path with a focus on maximizing value, preserving optionality, and managing execution risk.
