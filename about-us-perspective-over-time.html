How our world has changed

The 1990s

1990 to 1999 was a time of modernization and globalization when it came to science and technology. This sector saw huge growth and this decade brought about some of the biggest modern technological inventions that laid the building blocks of technology of today.

The 90’s also saw the advent of the world wide web, augmented by the introduction of Local Area Networks and Wide Area Networks. This time also saw the rise of Cisco in networking, HP & Dell in the desktop technology space, and EMC in storage. 1994 saw the launch of Amazon, and 1998 saw the launch of Google, which changed the IPO landscape through a one of a kind dutch auction based book building process for technology companies.

All the senior partners in the firm, except one, were in active corporate development or driving M&A and capital raise transactions during these times, ranging from selling mainframe solution companies to IBM and Cisco reseller partners, to information technology services deals, VAR market M&A, late stage semiconductor design and production company buyouts, and early stage capital raise deals for garage born Internet companies.

2000 – 2010

In this decade, we saw the rise of Amazon, Google and the World Wide Web, setting the foundations for the information/data age. Microsoft launched the .Net framework, social media was launched with Facebook, and towards the end of the decade the world saw a switch from desktop to mobile with the launch of the smartphone category.

During these times, almost all of the senior partners and more than half of our vice presidents were active in the M&A, capital raise and corporate development markets, working through the dotcom boom, two deep recessions, and several marquee cross border M&A deals. During this decade, S&L and its subsidiaries shifted their focus to growing Internet based solution firms, the new generation of digital marketing firms and mobile solution firms. Also during these times, S&L did not lay off a single employee or consultant across the globe and grew by high single digits percentages right through the crisis.

2010 – Present

We are in an exciting phase in the evolution of IT – the leap has been from products to the growth of data to the integration of products and data. SMAC based platform and solution firms are in demand, the cloud has become the way to deliver information technology, and Big data, analytics and the Internet of Things (IoT) have started to play a major role in bringing about the next wave of advancement. The second half of this decade has thrown up innovation with long term implications for the human race, through artificial intelligence, robotics and machine learning.

Our momentum post-recession has helped us move seamlessly into the second decade of this new millennium. We’ve embraced change, our domain experts have a thorough understanding of the next generation companies taking shape, and we have adopted technology to streamline our own operations – digital data dashboards, artificial intelligence in our regulatory compliance business, and next generation communication tools that have helped us create a robust global buy side ecosystem, reshaping the M&A landscape in Information Technology.
