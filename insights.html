WPP Acquires InfoSum: A Strategic Move into the Future of Data Collaboration and Privacy-First Marketing

7th April 2025

WPP has acquired InfoSum, a pioneer in data clean room technology — central infrastructure for redefining marketing in a post-cookie, privacy-regulated world.

In a move that signals the rapidly evolving landscape of data-driven marketing, WPP has acquired InfoSum, a pioneer in data clean room technology. InfoSum’s platform allows brands, media owners, and data providers to match customer datasets without ever moving or exposing the underlying data — solving one of the most pressing challenges in modern marketing: how to collaborate on consumer insights without breaching privacy regulations like GDPR and CCPA. At Sett & Lucas, we see this deal as a watershed moment marking the transition from performance marketing to privacy-respecting precision marketing.

SaaS Companies Valuation: How to Value and Sell a SaaS Business?

26th September 2023

The global SaaS market has been bullish for some time. We’re projecting high demand for quality SaaS businesses through the decade.

The global SaaS market has been bullish for some time now, with value projected to grow from $257 billion at the end of 2022 to $1298 billion by 2030. At Sett & Lucas, we believe the interest in SaaS businesses will consistently increase as innovation, availability of funds, and interest in the latest technologies rise. Key factors in valuing a SaaS business include churn rate, MRR/ARR, customer acquisition cost, customer lifetime value, net revenue retention, and revenue multiples — the most common valuation method in the industry.

New corporate tax regime – Boom or bust?

12th January 2018

Corporate taxes are now lower but not simpler, with mixed expectations about the effect on deficits, wages, and M&A activity.

Corporate taxes are now lower but not simpler. Changes include reducing the corporate tax rate from 35 percent to 21 percent, mandating a one-time repatriation of offshore profits, and a new 20 percent deduction for sole proprietors and partnerships. This extra corporate cash could drive mergers and acquisitions in mid-market technology, though high valuations may continue to put a damper on the largest transactions. Skeptics are concerned about deficits, executive pay, and a potential race to the bottom on global tax competition.
